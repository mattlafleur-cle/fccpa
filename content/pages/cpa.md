## Five questions behind financial clarity

Every Forest City CPA engagement starts with the same five questions to determine whether the financial information is reliable, useful, and ready to support better decisions.

REPORTING

Are your reports timely, accurate, understandable, and useful?

CASH

Can you clearly distinguish available cash from restricted, committed, or timing-related balances?

SYSTEMS

Do your accounting, payroll, banking, tax, and key financial systems work together reliably?

CONTROLS

Are approvals, review points, and financial safeguards appropriate for your organization?

INTERPRETATION

Can you use the numbers to make better decisions, not simply read them?

## How an engagement works

Every situation is different, but most relationships follow a simple path. We will tell you early whether we are a good fit and what the appropriate next step should be.

01

### Clarity Call

02

### Financial Assessment
