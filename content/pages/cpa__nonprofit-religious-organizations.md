Frequently Asked Questions

## Questions, answered.

Do you work with organizations outside of Cleveland and Northeast Ohio?+

Yes. Forest City CPA is rooted in Cleveland and Northeast Ohio, but we already serve clients remotely across the country. Modern cloud-accounting systems make it straightforward to support your organization wherever you are located.

Can you handle fund accounting for churches and synagogues?+

Yes. We provide specialized accounting for faith communities — including fund accounting, contribution reporting, clergy compensation planning, and board-ready financials tailored to the way religious organizations operate.

Do you prepare our organization for its annual audit?+

Yes. We deliver clean, audit-ready financials and organized documentation that make your annual audit or review smoother, faster, and less stressful for your staff and board.

What does a fractional CFO do for a nonprofit?+

A fractional CFO provides high-level strategic financial leadership — cash flow forecasting, KPI development, board reporting, and forward-looking guidance — without the cost of a full-time executive. It is an efficient way to bring senior financial leadership into your organization.

Can you help us track restricted funds and grants?+

Yes. Accurate tracking and reporting for restricted funds, grants, and donor designations is a core part of our nonprofit accounting work, so every dollar is accounted for and reported correctly.

How does an engagement begin?+

We start with a no-pressure conversation to understand your organization and where you feel blind spots. From there we assess your current accounting, design a scope built around your goals, and begin responsive ongoing support with direct access to experienced CPA leadership.

Will we work directly with a CPA?+

Yes. You have direct access to experienced, actively licensed CPA leadership — not a hand-off to a call center or a rotating cast of contacts.
