### What does Forest City do?

Forest City is a professional services firm built around three connected practice areas: artificial intelligence, CPA services, and advisory consulting. We help businesses, nonprofits, and leaders improve financial clarity, implement practical systems, and make better decisions.

### Is Forest City CPA replacing Cleveland CPAs and More?

Yes. Forest City CPA is the next evolution of Cleveland CPAs and More. The name is changing to better reflect the broader direction of the firm, but the underlying commitment to responsive, practical, and trusted service remains the same.

### Do you work with businesses outside Cleveland?

Yes. Forest City is rooted in Northeast Ohio, but we work with clients in other locations when the fit is right. Many services can be delivered remotely through secure cloud-based tools, video meetings, and shared systems.

### What types of clients do you serve?

We primarily serve small businesses, nonprofit organizations, churches, professional service firms, and leaders who need clearer financial information, better systems, and practical guidance.

### What is the difference between Forest City AI, Forest City CPA, and Forest City Consulting?

Forest City AI focuses on practical artificial intelligence training, workflow improvement, and implementation. Forest City CPA focuses on accounting, bookkeeping, tax, and fractional CFO services. Forest City Consulting focuses on strategy, operations, management consulting, and organizational improvement.

### Do you offer one-time projects or ongoing support?

Both. Some clients engage Forest City for a focused project, assessment, cleanup, or implementation. Others work with us on an ongoing basis for monthly accounting, advisory, AI support, or strategic consulting.

### How does a first consultation work?

The first step is a conversation to understand your organization, your current challenges, and what you are trying to build. From there, we determine whether Forest City is the right fit and recommend the most appropriate next step.
