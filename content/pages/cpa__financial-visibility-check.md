### [Questions — 5 steps x 2 questions; answer options: Yes / No / Not sure]

**Reporting**
1. Are your books up to date and your monthly reports ready within 10 days after month end?
2. Do your financial reports clearly show how your organization is performing?

**Cash**
3. Do you know how much cash is available after accounting for upcoming bills and any restricted funds?
4. Do you have a cash forecast showing expected money coming in and going out over the next 90 days?

**Systems**
5. Do your accounting, payroll, and banking systems work together reliably?
6. Do routine transactions flow into your books without repeated manual data entry?

**Controls**
7. Are there clear rules for who can approve spending and payments?
8. Does someone other than the person making payments review them?

**Decisions**
9. Do you regularly use your financial reports to decide what to change or prioritize?
10. Do you review your budget and financial plan on a regular schedule?

### [Recommendation per priority category]

- Reporting: Establish a monthly closing checklist and a consistent reporting date.
- Cash: Build a weekly cash forecast showing expected receipts, payments, and available cash.
- Systems: Map how transactions move between systems and identify repeated manual entry.
- Controls: Document spending approvals and establish an independent payment review appropriate to the organization’s size.
- Decisions: Schedule a monthly financial review covering results, budget differences, and upcoming decisions.
